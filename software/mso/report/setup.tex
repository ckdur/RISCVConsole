\subsection{Signal chain}
Figure~\ref{fig:bench} shows the bench. The MSO20 generates the stimulus with its DDS and
measures the same node with its oscilloscope channel~1, which is the voltage reference of all
the measurements. The front end converts it to a differential signal for the ADC. The ADC is
clocked and read by a RISC-V system (RISCVConsole) running on a ULX3S FPGA board, which stores
the samples in RAM; the host reads them back through JTAG.

\subsection{ADC interface}
The ADC connects to the ULX3S header through the ADCFIFO peripheral of RISCVConsole
(base address \texttt{0x64003000}):
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Signal & Direction & ULX3S pin & Function \\
\midrule
\texttt{valid} & FPGA $\to$ ADC & GN27 (E13) & Enables the conversions (control bit 0) \\
\texttt{rst\_n} & FPGA $\to$ ADC & GN26 (C13) & ADC reset: system reset or control bit 8 \\
\texttt{clk} & FPGA $\to$ ADC & GN25 (E14) & \SI{10}{MHz} conversion clock \\
\texttt{io\_valid} & ADC $\to$ FPGA & GN24 (D16) & A new result is ready \\
\texttt{bits[4:0]} & ADC $\to$ FPGA & GN23 \ldots\ GN19 & Result, MSB on GN23, LSB on GN19 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The results are captured on the ADC clock (control bit 16 selects the edge; the inverted edge is
used here) into an asynchronous FIFO of 8 entries that crosses into the \SI{50}{MHz} bus clock.
The firmware (\texttt{RISCVConsoleCode/src/adc.c}) enables the ADC, flushes the FIFO, then reads
the data register in a tight loop, storing \AdcSamples{} consecutive results in RAM. Bit 31 of the
data register flags an empty FIFO; the FIFO drops results when it is full, so the firmware also
counts the empty reads. In every capture of this report the empty reads are about a quarter of
all the reads, so the CPU outpaces the ADC and the record has no gaps. The capture duration
measured with the CLINT timer confirms the sample rate.

\subsection{Procedure}
Each measurement (\texttt{adc\_run.sh}) configures and enables the DDS, captures the scope, boots
the capture firmware through JTAG (\texttt{jtag-boot.sh}), logs its UART report, and dumps
\texttt{adc\_buf} through JTAG while the DDS keeps running. \texttt{adc\_sweep.sh} runs a ramp
and then the sine at every frequency of the sweep; \texttt{report/make\_report.py} analyzes the
runs and builds this document.
