\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Device:} \AdcBits-bit differential SAR ADC, clocked at \SI{10}{MHz}
        (one conversion every 7 clocks, $f_s = \SI{\AdcFs}{MS/s}$).
  \item \textbf{Digital interface:} ADCFIFO peripheral of RISCVConsole on a ULX3S (ECP5-85F)
        board. Samples cross into the \SI{50}{MHz} bus clock through an asynchronous FIFO and the
        CPU stores \AdcSamples{} consecutive samples in RAM, dumped afterwards through JTAG.
  \item \textbf{Stimulus:} DDS channel 1 of a Vimu MSO20 (VmMsoOpenSDK), driven single-ended.
  \item \textbf{Front end:} single-ended to differential conversion stage.
        % TODO: describe the circuit (topology, components, gain, common mode)
  \item \textbf{Voltage reference for the measurement:} MSO20 CH1 on the DDS output, DC coupled,
        \SI{\pm 1}{V} range.
\end{itemize}}
