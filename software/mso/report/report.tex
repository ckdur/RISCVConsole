\section{Method}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Static linearity:} histogram test with a triangle of \SI{\RampFreq}{Hz}
        (10 periods in the capture) from \SIrange{\RampVmin}{\RampVmax}{V}, overdriving both ends
        of the range (overdriven: \RampOverdriven). With a linear input, the number of hits of a code
        is proportional to its width; the transition levels $T_k$ follow from the cumulative histogram
        and the ramp ends measured on the scope. DNL and INL use the end-point line through
        $T_1$ and $T_{\AdcLastCode}$.
  \item \textbf{Dynamic performance:} sine of \SI{\SineAmplMv}{mV} peak-to-peak setting and
        \SI{\SineBiasMv}{mV} bias, at frequencies with an odd prime number of periods in the capture.
        SINAD comes from a 4-parameter sine fit (residual including quantization, distortion and
        noise), $\text{ENOB} = (\text{SINAD} - 1.76)/6.02$. Harmonics, THD, SNR and SFDR come from a
        Blackman--Harris windowed spectrum, with harmonics folded into the first Nyquist zone.
  \item \textbf{Input amplitude:} a sine fit of the scope capture gives the real amplitude at the
        DDS output for each frequency, to separate a stimulus roll-off from the ADC response.
\end{itemize}
